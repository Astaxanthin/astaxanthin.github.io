Xiao Zhou (周晓)

Hi, I am an Associate Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Before joining SJTU, I spent two and a half years as a postdoctoral researcher at the Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory.

I received my PhD from the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University. I obtained both my bachelor's and master's degrees from the Department of Automation, Xi'an Jiaotong University.

My research interests focus on medical artificial intelligence, particularly AI for cancer diagnosis and treatment. My work covers areas including computational pathology, multi-omics, and related topics in biomedical AI.

I am currently recruiting PhD students for 2027. If you are interested, please feel free to contact me via email at zhou_xiao@sjtu.edu.cn.
